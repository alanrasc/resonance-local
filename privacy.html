Resonance: Offline MP3 & Video Player — Privacy policy

Also known as Resonance Local. Published by Northcom LTD. Effective date and last updated: 31 October 2026. This Play edition has no ads or advertising SDK. Optional one-time theme purchases use Google Play Billing. No Resonance account is required.

Local media and app data

Resonance reads audio files and their metadata to show your library and play media. You can choose other files using Android's file picker. Playlists, bookmarks, lyrics and timings, listening history, playback positions, preferences and processing-job information are stored on your device. Conversion, audio effects and vocal/instrumental separation process selected media on the device. This version has no transcription or translation service and no user account system.

Microphone and permissions

Microphone permission is requested for recording your voice. Recording starts when you choose Record and uses a visible foreground-service notification; stop it in the app or its recording notification. Recordings are saved in private app storage unless you export/share them. Sound from speakers or the surrounding room can be picked up by the microphone. Media-library permission lets the app list audio; older Android versions use storage permission. Notifications show playback, recording and processing status. Foreground services and wake locks let requested playback or processing continue in the background. You can revoke permissions in Android app settings; related features may then stop working.

Network requests and third parties

This local-media edition has no media-link streaming, media downloading or embedded website player. Optional separation-model downloads contact GitHub and its delivery infrastructure. These services receive your IP address, request headers and timing. HTTPS protects transport but does not make requests anonymous. Provider policies apply. Optional theme purchases are described below.

Reports and support

There is no automatic report upload. Saving future crash details is off by default. If enabled, the latest saved report contains the exception type and a limited stack trace, not exception messages or raw system logs. Device information and saved diagnostics are separate optional report fields, off by default. Your complaint is included as typed: remove private information before sharing. Preview the report, then choose its recipient using Android's sharing menu. The chosen sharing app and recipient process what you send under their own arrangements. Do not include passwords, private media or identifying information that is unnecessary to explain a problem.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local settings and library records are retained until you edit, clear or reset them. Private session prevents new listening history from being added; it does not erase existing history. Clear history in Listening history removes its records. Report a bug lets you clear saved diagnostics; turning crash-detail saving off also clears the saved crash file. Use the relevant tools to delete lyrics or recordings and the file manager to delete exported/shared files. Model downloads and interrupted processing jobs may use temporary device storage until completed, cancelled/discarded or app data is cleared.

For a complete local reset, use Android Settings → Apps → Resonance → Storage → Clear data/storage, or uninstall. This removes private app data, including recordings saved only inside Resonance, lyrics, settings, playlists and models. Back up anything you want to keep first. Exported backups, shared media and downloads in shared storage may survive; delete these separately. Exported backups contain library information and lyrics and are not encrypted by Resonance. Android cloud/device-transfer backup is disabled for the app; user-created exports are separate.

Email a deletion request

This covers support emails, reports and attachments held by Northcom and its service providers. We will assess the request and respond without undue delay, normally within one month where UK data protection law applies. If an applicable exception or extension is needed, we will explain it. Requests to Northcom do not automatically erase those independent records. We remain responsible for handling requests about data we control and involving our processors where required.

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use these to answer you, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. For ordinary support, we rely on our legitimate interests in responding to requests and fixing defects. Processing needed to meet applicable legal duties uses the relevant legal obligation. Support retention is determined by whether a request is unresolved, whether correspondence is needed for a related defect investigation or follow-up, and whether records are needed to meet a legal duty or handle an actual dispute. Your privacy rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Rights have legal conditions and exceptions. Where consent is used, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. We may ask for proportionate information to verify a request, not unnecessary identity documents. Advertising, purchases and children

Google Play handles checkout and payment details. Resonance queries product prices and ownership and caches verified purchase records locally to unlock paid themes and restore access for the same Play account. It does not receive your card number. There are no rewarded trials, advertising identifiers requested for ads, or ad-removal offers in this edition. The intended audience is people aged 13 and above.

Changes to data handling must be reflected here and in the public privacy policy and store disclosures before they take effect. The app's policy remains available in Settings without opening a webpage.
