Resonance: Offline MP3 & Video Player — Privacy policy

Also known as Resonance Local. Published by Northcom LTD. Effective date and last updated: 9 October 2026. This policy covers the local-media Android app, including its Google advertising and purchase integration where enabled. Test versions use test advertisements, which can still involve data processing. Publishing this policy does not mean the app has launched or that live ads and purchases are available.

Local media and app data

Resonance reads audio files and their metadata to show your library and play media. You can choose other files using Android's file picker. Playlists, bookmarks, lyrics and timings, listening history, playback positions, preferences and processing-job information are stored on your device. Conversion, audio effects and vocal/instrumental separation process selected media on the device. This version has no transcription or translation service and no user account system.

Theme trials

Theme trials store the theme, remaining trial time and local clock/reboot counters privately on this device. These records prevent repeating a consumed trial and are excluded from app exports and Android backup. They do not contain a user account or device identifier and are not uploaded by Resonance. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes them. Optional rewarded ads contact Google under the advertising disclosures below, only after the user requests a trial.

Microphone and permissions

Microphone permission is requested for recording your voice. Recording starts when you choose Record and uses a visible foreground-service notification; stop it in the app or its recording notification. Recordings are saved in private app storage unless you export/share them. Sound from speakers or the surrounding room can be picked up by the microphone. Media-library permission lets the app list audio; older Android versions use storage permission. Notifications show playback, recording and processing status. Foreground services and wake locks let requested playback or processing continue in the background. You can revoke permissions in Android app settings; related features may then stop working.

Network requests and third parties

This local-media edition has no media-link streaming, media downloading or embedded website player. Optional separation-model downloads contact GitHub and its delivery infrastructure. These services receive your IP address, request headers and timing. HTTPS protects transport but does not make requests anonymous. Provider policies apply. Advertising and purchase requests, where included, are described below.

Reports and support

There is no automatic report upload. Saving future crash details is off by default. If enabled, the latest saved report contains the exception type and a limited stack trace, not exception messages or raw system logs. Device information and saved diagnostics are separate optional report fields, off by default. Your complaint is included as typed: remove private information before sharing. Preview the report, then choose its recipient using Android's sharing menu. The chosen sharing app and recipient process what you send under their own arrangements. Do not include passwords, private media or identifying information that is unnecessary to explain a problem.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local settings and library records are retained until you edit, clear or reset them. Private session prevents new listening history from being added; it does not erase existing history. Clear history in Listening history removes its records. Report a bug lets you clear saved diagnostics; turning crash-detail saving off also clears the saved crash file. Use the relevant tools to delete lyrics or recordings and the file manager to delete exported/shared files. Model downloads and interrupted processing jobs may use temporary device storage until completed, cancelled/discarded or app data is cleared.

For a complete local reset, use Android Settings → Apps → Resonance → Storage → Clear data/storage, or uninstall. This removes private app data, including recordings saved only inside Resonance, lyrics, settings, playlists and models. Back up anything you want to keep first. Exported backups, shared media and downloads in shared storage may survive; delete these separately. Exported backups contain library information and lyrics and are not encrypted by Resonance. Android cloud/device-transfer backup is disabled for the app; user-created exports are separate.

Information already shared with support cannot be recalled by clearing your phone. Contact northcom@northcomtech.com to request access, correction or deletion of information the publisher has received. Requests concerning a media provider must be directed to that provider.

Advertising, purchases and children

The Google-enabled edition includes Google Mobile Ads, Google Play Billing and User Messaging Platform (UMP). The SDK-free edition does not. Google-enabled builds contact UMP at startup to refresh privacy requirements and display the configured consent message where required. Ad privacy choices are available alongside the Privacy policy in Settings. Debug builds use Google's test ad identifiers. Test ads can still transmit data.

Declining optional advertising data use does not remove local settings, playlists, lyrics, playback positions or the record of your privacy choices. It does not grant an ad-removal purchase. Ad requests are made only when UMP permits them under the current choices. Limited ads may still appear and involve data processing; declining is not a promise that no information reaches Google. The consent message offers Consent, Do not consent and Manage options. You can revisit Ad privacy choices in Settings to manage or withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

Google's advertising SDK can collect IP addresses (which may indicate general location), device/account identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Consent signals and applicable privacy choices affect requests; declining personalised advertising does not necessarily mean no data processing. Resonance does not intentionally supply song titles, lyrics, recordings or library contents to its ads integration. Advertiser pages have their own policies. Google privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. SDK disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

Opening the store/test centre contacts Google Play for products and ownership. With a configured billing key, the Google variant can check ownership when applying paid themes and returning to the foreground. Purchase receipts are kept privately outside Android backup to support offline access and restoration. Google handles payment details; Resonance does not collect card numbers or bank credentials. Clearing app data deletes local receipts, not Google's purchase records. Use Google's account/privacy controls for information held by Google.

Intended audience: teenagers and adults aged 13 and over. The app is not intended for children under 13. Age-related protections and the age at which a young person can consent vary by location; being over 13 does not waive those protections. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact northcom@northcomtech.com so we can investigate and address it.

We rely on consent where required for optional advertising storage, identifiers and personalisation. Handling purchases is necessary to provide purchased features. Security and fraud prevention may rely on legitimate interests or legal obligations where applicable; Google's consent message and privacy notice explain the purposes and bases used by its services. Core local playback does not require consent to optional advertising data use.
